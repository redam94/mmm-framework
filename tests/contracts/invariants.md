# The invariants the src/ refactor must not move

This is the numbered invariant table required by `technical-docs/src-refactor.md` §2.1
(PR 0.1). The fingerprint matrix (`tests/contracts/model_matrix.py` /
`tests/test_graph_fingerprint.py`) references invariants **by id** — a golden case is
justified by the invariant it covers — and an implementer cannot otherwise tell whether
their case covers "inv. 12". Every row states the invariant in one line, the `file:line`
in `src/` (or `tests/`) where the behaviour lives, and the test that pins it — or `none`,
which is exactly the information the fingerprint matrix needs.

**Rules of this table:**

* **Rows may be added; rows are never silently deleted.** Retiring an invariant is a
  reviewed edit that replaces the row's text with a strikethrough and the PR that retired
  it — the ids are load-bearing references from the fingerprint goldens and from
  `technical-docs/src-refactor.md`.
* "Pinned by: none (this PR's fingerprint matrix)" means the graph fingerprint
  (`tests/contracts/graph_fingerprint.py` — `free_RVs`, `logp_terms`,
  `deterministic_values` with its load-bearing abs-sum) is the intended and only pin;
  the matrix must carry a case for that row.
* Line numbers were re-verified on this checkout (2026-08-06, after #281–#300 landed).
  The spec's §2.1 was written at v1.3.3 and several of its cites have drifted; the
  numbers below supersede them. Line numbers rot — trust the symbol names first.

**An honest count.** The spec's headline is "thirty documented byte-identical
invariants". Harvesting every byte-identical / bit-stable claim the spec states with a
`file:line` yields the **39** rows below — more than thirty because the four estimand
equivalences are split one per row, and because four of the spec's §8 bugs have since
been fixed (#281–#286) and their fixes hardened into *new* pinned invariants (windowed
refusal, interval-mass provenance, the canonical ROI reader, the shared period table)
that now bind this refactor just as hard. Ids 19 and 26 match the two the spec names by
number, and the spec's third, unnumbered no-pin row is inv. 39; nothing here is padding — every row cites the spec section or the shipped fix it
comes from.
